I'm Alexis Delevett, Adapted Technology Specialist at the College of San Mateo Disability Resource Center. I work alongside students, faculty, and colleagues to remove access barriers and build practical paths into learning.

Technology is most effective when it fits the person and the context. I work one-on-one with students to identify technologies that address disability-related barriers and support their academic goals. That work includes listening, testing, training, and following up as students put new workflows into practice.

I also collaborate with faculty and colleagues on accessible course materials, classroom technology, alternate media, and workshops that make accessibility easier to understand and apply.
