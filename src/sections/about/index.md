As the Adapted Technology Specialist at CSM, I prioritize access by working with students to identify technologies that meet their individual needs and allow them to perform to their capabilities. In addition, through my support of the Alternate Media program, adapted technology services can become the last line of defense to ensure the student's learning materials are accessible and available for use with their assistive technologies.

This "access first" approach can be perceived as competing with developmental learning goals given the evolving landscape and general availability of artificial intelligence (AI) assistants especially. I hope to contribute to a nuanced discussion of technologies in service of access and participation by listening to students, faculty and staff alike and working to come to a shared understanding of technology accommodations and their role in learning.
