As the Adapted Technology Specialist at CSM, I provide students with access to technologies that level the playing field and allow them to perform to their capabilities. In addition, especially through my support of the Alternate Media program, adapted technology services can become the last line of defense to ensure the student's learning materials are accessible and available for use with their assistive technologies. This dual responsibility to ensure access to technologies & to ensure they work with the students' instructional materials leads me to work closely with faculty & staff to raise awareness about the importance of accessible instructional materials and which technologies may be used to access them.

If I had to try and put this approach into a teaching philosophy, I think it's fair to say that I believe that "access comes first".
