I'm Alexis Delevett, Adapted Technology Specialist at the College of San Mateo Disability Resource Center. I work one-on-one and in small group settings with students to identify technologies that address disability-related barriers and support their academic goals.

I also collaborate with faculty and colleagues on accessible course materials, educational technology, alternate media, and workshops to demystify accessibility and promote universal access.
