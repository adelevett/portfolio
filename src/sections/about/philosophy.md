## Start with the barrier

The goal is not to recommend the most impressive tool. It is to understand what is getting in the way — for this student, in this course, on this device.

## Practice in context

Technology training works best with the real course, document, device, and task a person needs to complete. Abstract demos fade quickly; a workflow rehearsed against an actual assignment tends to stick.

## Build independence

A useful workflow should reduce effort over time and give the person more control over their learning. Success looks like a student who needs me less each semester — and who can explain their own tools to someone else.
