I am privileged to work with an awesome team at the DRC and am eager to contribute by participating in department-wide initiatives such as our annual priority enrollment program inviting high school seniors on campus to learn about DRC resources. Whenever possible I table at campus outreach events such as the health and veterans resource fairs.

## Committee assignments

I participate in the DRC Scholarship Committee reviewing student eligibility and submissions for award consideration. As part of my campus-wide service, I regularly join the distance education advisory committee (DEAC) to stay abreast of developments impacting digital accessibility on campus. In addition, I contribute to the campus Accessibility Capability Maturity Model (ACMM) workgroups on Universal Design for Learning (UDL) and Equally Effective Alternative Access Plan (EEAAP) and collaborate with the district Open Educational Resource (OER) community on the Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) Task Force.
