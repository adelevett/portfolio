I am privileged to work with an awesome team at the DRC and am eager to contribute by participating in department-wide initiatives such as our annual priority enrollment program inviting high school seniors on campus to learn about DRC resources. Whenever possible I table at campus outreach events such as the health and veterans resource fairs.

## Committee assignments

I contribute to the DRC Scholarship Committee by reviewing student eligibility and submissions for award consideration. As part of my campus-wide service, I regularly join the distance education advisory committee (DEAC) to stay abreast of developments impacting digital accessibility on campus. In addition, I am looking forward to adding my perspective as part of the campus Accessibility Capability Maturity Model (ACMM) work groups on Universal Design for Learning (UDL) and Equally Effective Alternative Access Plan (EEAAP). Finally I collaborate with the district Open Educational Resource (OER) community on the Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) Task Force and meet with members of the campus community to discuss the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Ethics and Accessibility.
