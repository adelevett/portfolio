I contribute to campus and district conversations where disability services, teaching, technology, and emerging tools meet.

## Committee and campus service

Scholarship Committee work supports student scholarship processes. The District Teaching & Learning Committee is where I contribute to district-wide teaching, learning, and conference conversations. Inside the DRC, day-to-day team service covers staff coordination, orientations, and the program operations that keep student support running.

## Cross-campus and professional collaboration

I have developed accessibility workshops with colleagues at other colleges, including a collaboration with Napa Valley College, and participated in Student Success Link initiatives that connect students to support earlier. Conference proposals and professional exchange through CAPED keep me in conversation with the wider assistive technology community. Workgroup and research conversations on AI and assistive technology include data-policy questions raised by AI services in student-facing work.
