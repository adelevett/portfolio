I meet with students to understand the barrier first, then identify and practice a sustainable technology workflow. Follow-up is part of the work: a tool is only useful when it fits the student's course, device, and way of learning.

Since joining the DRC, this has meant hundreds of one-on-one consultations and structured follow-up messages, so that no student leaves with a tool they haven't rehearsed on their own coursework.
