I am privileged to primarily meet with students one-on-one, and often as one of the first contacts on campus. My goal is to ensure the student feels supported in their current strategies while encouraging them to coonsider alternatives and be open to changing demands especially concerning the transition from high school to college. 

## Adapted Technology

Adapted technology can include specialized tools, built-in accessibility features, or custom solutions.

- Note-taking and writing support: Dragon Dictation, Smartpens, Otter AI, digital recorders
- Reading and text-to-speech: Natural Reader, Kurzweil, built-in screen readers and read aloud features
- Everyday accessibility: the features already on students' phones, tablets, and laptops

## Alternate Media

Students with qualifying print disabilities may receive learning materials in alternate formats. In partnership with the Alternate Media Specialist, I work with students to help navigate the alt media request process an dprepare them to access their materials in their preferred format.

- Student training and orientation: Working one-on-one or in group settings when possible I introduce students to available technologies and support them as they familiarize themselves with the functionality
- Collaboration with Alt Media Specialist: Request tracking and follow-ups as well as special case handling for unusual formats or accommodation needs
- Campus outreach and coordination: Working with campus Bookstore and OER teams to encourage faculty to submit timely textbook adoption paperwork and consider accesisbility implications of their instructional materials

### Working with faculty

Faculty share a responsibility in ensuring classroom materials are accessible and making information about required learning materials available for review in the CSM Bookstore's dashboard.
