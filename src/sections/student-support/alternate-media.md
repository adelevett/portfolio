Students with qualifying print disabilities may receive learning materials in alternate formats. In partnership with the Alternate Media Specialist, I help prepare course materials in formats students can actually use, and train students to access those materials with confidence.

## The workflow

- **Coordination of alternate-format requests** from intake through delivery, including status tracking during high-volume periods like the start of term and winter break
- **Accessible PDF and ePub workflows**, including review of scans, readings, textbooks, and course files
- **Publisher and library sources**, including Bookshare, when a ready-made accessible edition exists
- **Student training** so converted materials are opened, navigated, and listened to effectively with tools like Natural Reader and Kurzweil

## Working with faculty

Alternate media is also a faculty-facing service. I respond to textbook and course-material questions, help faculty understand what makes a source file convertible, and flag accessibility issues before they reach students. The earlier a course file is fixed, the fewer emergency conversions happen at week one.
